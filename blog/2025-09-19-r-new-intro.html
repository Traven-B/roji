Roji is a port of the Pixyll theme to the Middleman static site generator. Its Liquid templates were rewritten in Embedded Ruby (ERB), with additional support for categories and tags. The project began as a personal exploration of Middleman’s flexibility, while honoring Pixyll’s minimalist design “crafted with <3” by John Otander.

We have conventions and small automation scripts for:

  • Data-driven content
  • Page-specific styling
  • Image management

Roji organizes images, data, and stylesheets per post. Images and data live in directories under images/posts/ and data/posts/, named after the post. Optional per-post stylesheets are single files, also named for the post, tucked away in a dedicated subdirectory under assets/stylesheets/ allowing Middleman to compile them without cluttering the main theme. Additional stylesheets for non-post pages can also be placed in additional_styles.

Small automation scripts help streamline common tasks: do_images.rb manages post images, use_data.rb sets up post-specific data files, and find_widows.rb identifies orphaned assets for cleanup.

Kramdown’s flexibility allows classes, IDs, and other attributes to be applied to block- or span-level markdown elements, and even supports markdown inside HTML tags.

For detailed setup and examples, see the Roji in Action guide.
